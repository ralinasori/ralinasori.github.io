rclone-backup

rclone-backup is a small personal tool used by one developer (ralinasori) to copy their own game development files from their own computers to their own Google Drive, using the open-source rclone program.

It is not a public service. It has no sign-up, no other users, and no servers. It only touches the Google Drive of the person who runs it.
